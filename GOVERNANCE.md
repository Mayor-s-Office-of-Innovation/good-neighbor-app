# Governance

Good Neighbor App is maintained by the Mayor's Office of Innovation in collaboration with City technology partners.

## Roles

- Product owner: accountable for scope, priority, and public-service outcomes.
- Technical owner: accountable for architecture, operations, and SDLC compliance.
- Maintainers: review and merge code, infrastructure, and documentation changes.
- Contributors: propose changes through pull requests.

## Decisions

Material architecture decisions are recorded in `docs/adr/`. Security, privacy, data classification, and production-readiness decisions must be documented before launch.

## Reviews

All pull requests require review. Infrastructure, authentication, authorization, data model, and deployment changes require a maintainer with relevant ownership.
